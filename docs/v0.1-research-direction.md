# v0.1 Experiment - Sleep / Activity Mouse

## Status

Umwelt 0.1 implements the first complete recorded-to-synthetic computational
experiment. It is an inspectable baseline and reproducibility scaffold, not a
validated account of mouse sleep regulation.

## Core question

> Which minimal explicit temporal mechanisms reproduce selected properties of
> recorded mouse sleep/activity dynamics beyond aggregate occupancy?

The experiment makes this question executable. It does not assume the answer is
yes, and it preserves discrepancies as first-class output.

## Recorded dataset

The selected source is the COMPASS data deposit:

- title: *PIR data and EEG scoring for Wellcome Open Research methods paper
  (Brown et al 2016)*;
- authors: Laurence A. Brown, Sibah Hasan, Russell G. Foster, and Stuart N.
  Peirson;
- dataset DOI: [`10.5281/zenodo.160344`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.160344);
- related article DOI:
  [`10.12688/wellcomeopenres.9892.2`](https://doi.org/10.12688/wellcomeopenres.9892.2);
- dataset license: CC0 1.0;
- selected files: `24mice_activity_LD1week.csv` and
  `24mice_sleep_LD1week.csv`.

The selected files contain seven days of 10-second observations for 24 male
C57BL/6J mice. The animals were individually housed in four published groups of
six cages under a 12-hour light / 12-hour dark schedule, with the light period
beginning at time zero.

The activity values are passive infrared sensor measurements. The sleep labels
are behaviorally defined from at least 40 seconds of immobility. They are not
the EEG scoring contained in separate files in the same deposit. Umwelt records
this distinction in provenance and does not reinterpret the weekly labels as
direct physiological measurements.

## Integrity and local storage

The adapter pins the record's exact filenames, byte sizes, and MD5 checksums.
Downloads are streamed to temporary partial files, verified, and then moved into
place. Existing valid files are reused; mismatched files require explicit repair
mode. Raw data remains under ignored `data/raw/` storage.

MD5 is used here only to verify equality with hashes published by the source
repository. Completed Umwelt run artifacts receive SHA-256 hashes.

## Subject split and exposure caveat

The default experiment uses a subject-level holdout:

- training: subjects `1-5`, `7-11`, `13-17`, and `19-23`;
- held-out development: subjects `6`, `12`, `18`, and `24`.

One subject is held out from each published group of six cages. This avoids
placing one entire acquisition group exclusively in development evaluation. It
does not create a randomized biological treatment comparison and cannot
eliminate all batch, cage, sensor, or individual effects.

The original baseline results for these four subjects were inspected before the
duration-aware model ladder was specified. They therefore remain excluded from
fitting but are not a pristine confirmatory test set. New reports call them
held-out development subjects and restrict conclusions to model-development
evidence.

The model sees no held-out state or activity values during fitting. During
generation it receives only the held-out timestamp grids and missingness masks,
so recorded and synthetic metrics cover corresponding durations and source gaps.

## Explicit model ladder

The original `phase-conditioned-markov-v1` remains runnable. The expanded
comparison uses two explicit mechanism switches:

- daily phase conditioning: off or on;
- elapsed bout-duration conditioning: off or on.

This produces four state models:

1. `state-markov-v1`: pooled first-order state persistence;
2. `phase-conditioned-markov-v1`: the original daily-phase baseline;
3. `duration-hazard-v1`: a discrete elapsed-duration hazard without phase;
4. `phase-duration-hazard-v1`: phase and elapsed-duration conditioning.

Duration-aware models use fixed configurable epoch bins and shrink sparse-cell
hazards toward pooled state/phase hazards. The canonical bins and priors are
declared before execution and are not tuned against development results.

All four models share one fitted activity-emission mechanism containing:

- two observable computational states: `wake` and `sleep`;
- 24 configurable daily phase bins by default;
- phase- and state-conditioned zero-inflated gamma activity emissions;
- empirical positive-activity caps taken only from training observations;
- pooled parameters shared across training subjects;
- explicit master and per-series random seeds.

Each model generates new states and activity measurements. None classifies the
held-out observations or contains a learned representation of subjective state.
The factorial comparison attributes changed state dynamics to phase and/or
duration conditioning while keeping emissions constant.

## Individual-variation experiment

The temporal model ladder exposed a separate question: a pooled model may match
population-level temporal structure while generating synthetic individuals that
remain too similar to one another. Umwelt therefore implements a second,
strictly scoped v0.1 development experiment rather than silently making the
phase+duration model more complicated.

The scientific question is:

> Is training-population variation in a minimal set of state-dynamics
> parameters sufficient to reproduce between-mouse heterogeneity that a pooled
> phase+duration model misses?

The experimental population model keeps `phase-duration-hazard-v1` as its base.
For every training mouse it derives two sustained smoothed offsets relative to the
pooled training dynamics:

- a sleep-bias logit offset, reconstructed to increase wake-to-sleep hazard and
  decrease sleep-to-wake hazard when positive;
- a state-switching logit offset applied in the same direction to both leaving
  hazards.

A generated synthetic individual samples one training-derived profile uniformly
with replacement and keeps that profile fixed for its full generated series.
The profile shifts the pooled state probabilities while phase conditioning,
elapsed-duration conditioning, duration bins, smoothing, and activity emissions
remain unchanged.

This is deliberately an empirical population baseline rather than a fitted
continuous hierarchical distribution. It asks whether a very small source of
stable between-subject variation is sufficient before introducing a more
complex population model.

The held-out development animals are never used to estimate profile values or
to select which profile a synthetic animal receives. Pooled and population
variants use paired state random streams within each subject/replicate. Activity
uses a separate matched seed, while profile selection uses another deterministic
seed. This reduces irrelevant Monte Carlo differences without allowing
activity-emission draws or the development animal to alter state generation.

The implementation does not claim that these offsets are biological traits,
personality, sleep need, intention, or physiology. They are computational
population parameters whose usefulness must be judged only by recorded-versus-
synthetic comparisons.

The experiment is exposed through:

```console
umwelt individual --config examples/v0.1-temporal-lab.json --output runs/v0.1-individual-variation
```

## Canonical individual-variation result

The canonical comparison used 32 replicates per model and the four declared
development subjects, producing 256 synthetic weekly series. The complete
[report](../runs/v0.1-individual-variation/report.md), compact metrics, seeds,
provenance, fitted parameters, diagnostics, and artifact checksums are retained
for review.

The result is mixed and informative:

- the recorded value fell inside the population model's predictive interval for
  four of six declared heterogeneity summaries, compared with none for the
  pooled control;
- median between-subject sleep-fraction SD increased from `0.0093` to `0.0288`
  toward the recorded `0.0468`;
- median state-changes-per-hour SD increased from `0.3815` to `2.0670` toward
  the recorded `2.8765`;
- short- and longer-lag sleep autocorrelation remained broadly similar to the
  pooled model;
- median wake-bout Wasserstein distance worsened from `22.25 s` to `46.62 s`,
  and sleep-bout distance worsened from `37.02 s` to `49.93 s`;
- both sleep and activity phase-profile RMSE were larger for the population
  variant in every paired replicate.

The two-offset mechanism therefore demonstrates that training-derived stable
variation can recover part of the missing population spread, but it is not a
better general replacement for the pooled phase+duration model. Increasing
heterogeneity exposes a trade-off with temporal and circadian fidelity that a
future population model would need to address explicitly.

This evidence is sufficient to begin planning v0.2 because v0.1 now provides a
reproducible laboratory that can reveal both improvements and failures. This is
a project-scope decision, not scientific validation of the model. Prospective
subject-level evaluation, individual activity emissions, and the remaining bout
and long-lag discrepancies are explicitly deferred. No v0.2 capability is
implemented by this experiment.

## Evaluation

Recorded and synthetic summaries are computed separately before comparison.
The original metrics remain available. The replicated temporal comparison adds:

- wake and sleep occupancy;
- between-subject sleep-fraction variation;
- all-epoch, wake, and positive activity distributions;
- nonzero activity fraction;
- transition counts, probabilities, and state changes per hour;
- wake and sleep bout-duration summaries plus empirical one-dimensional
  Wasserstein and Kolmogorov-Smirnov distances;
- daily phase profiles for sleep fraction and mean activity;
- sleep-state autocorrelation at configured lags;
- phase-profile root mean square differences;
- per-subject metrics for each development mouse;
- deterministic replicate medians and configurable predictive simulation
  intervals;
- model-family tables and dependency-free SVG diagnostics.

The individual-variation experiment additionally reports descriptive
between-subject standard deviations and ranges for selected state/activity
summaries, plus per-subject predictive intervals. These are diagnostics of
population spread, not estimates of a biological variance component.

No scientific acceptance threshold is currently preregistered, so the software
does not label a run as validated or failed. Predictive simulation intervals
describe stochastic model output and are not confidence intervals.

## Reproducible outputs

Both experiment schemas refuse to overwrite an existing output directory. The
expanded temporal comparison and individual-variation experiment record:

1. the resolved configuration;
2. input DOI, license, authorship, context, paths, sizes, and checksums;
3. training and held-out development subject roles and the exposure caveat;
4. Umwelt version or Git revision information where available;
5. all fitted model parameters and assumptions;
6. the master seed and every derived synthetic-series seed;
7. compact replicate metrics, without retaining every full trajectory;
8. aggregate and subject-level predictive summaries;
9. readable reports and deterministic SVG diagnostics;
10. SHA-256 hashes for all completed run artifacts.

The individual-variation artifacts additionally preserve which training profile
was sampled for each synthetic subject and replicate.

## Expected limitations

The first-order baseline matches some aggregate rates while failing on important
bout-duration and longer-lag structure. Duration conditioning is still a compact
descriptive hazard, not a biological mechanism. Pooling subjects can hide
individual variation; the empirical population experiment now makes that
failure testable but does not assume its two-offset mechanism is sufficient.
Discrete phase bins simplify circadian dynamics. Independent activity emissions
omit serial dependence in activity magnitude. Behaviorally defined sleep from
immobility is not detailed sleep staging.

These limitations are part of the experiment. A future model should become more
complex only in response to a defined question or a documented baseline
failure, not merely because additional complexity is available.

## Scientific boundary

Recorded labels are measurements or derived annotations with provenance.
Synthetic states and individual profiles are variables of explicit models.
Neither category provides direct evidence of subjective experience, true
intention, emotion, consciousness, personality, or complete biological state.
