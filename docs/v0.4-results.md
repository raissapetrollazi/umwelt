# v0.4 first social-model result

**Decision:** S1 met the [pre-declared preference rule](v0.4-frozen-experiment.md)
on both development and the once-run held-out CalMS21 Task 1 test. This is a
measured improvement over S0 in this image-space experiment. It does not
validate either model as a biological account of social behavior.

| Equal-pair mean discrepancy | Development: S0 → S1 | Held-out test: S0 → S1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Pair-distance empirical W1 | 132.96 → 114.58 px | 144.27 → 100.57 px |
| Adjacent signed-distance-change empirical W1 | 2.347 → 2.338 px | 2.135 → 2.131 px |
| Pairs with lower distance error under S1 | 10 / 14 (required 9) | 13 / 19 (required 12) |

The held-out distance discrepancy fell 30.3% relative to S0. The signed-change
discrepancy fell only 0.2%, so this experiment gives little evidence of better
short-term distance dynamics. S1 worsened distance discrepancy on six of the
19 test pairs. Its held-out mean distance discrepancy is still 100.57 image
pixels; the median across test pairs of their observed mean pair distance is
about 274.93 px. The models remain approximate, especially in absolute
geometric fidelity. The rule's 5% tolerance on signed change was met, but that
criterion is not a claim of meaningful improvement in that metric.

The model was fit on 56 outcome-blind selected training sequences and scored on
14 other training sequences before the provided 19-sequence test split was
opened for outcomes. Eight paired-seed synthetic replicates were generated per
pair and model; pairs, not frames or replicates, received equal weight. No
first-frame pair was excluded. The fitted resident directional term was
`+0.3320` px per frame; its sign is a model association, not evidence of an
approach drive. The canonical test used the unchanged development fit and
software revision `1aadcb58cbd20b62d8f7f66ea04ddff00be4172c`.

The compact per-pair and replicate artifacts are generated locally in
`runs/v0.4-social-development/` and `runs/v0.4-social-heldout-test/` and are
excluded from Git. The model, score, seed, exposure, source, revision, and
artifact hashes are recorded there. Reproduce them from the verified local
archive with:

```console
umwelt social-develop \
  --archive data/raw/calms21/task1_classic_classification.zip \
  --output runs/v0.4-social-development
umwelt social-test \
  --archive data/raw/calms21/task1_classic_classification.zip \
  --development runs/v0.4-social-development \
  --output runs/v0.4-social-heldout-test
```

Each output directory must be new. The test command checks the development
artifact hashes, source SHA-256, frozen protocol hash, and Git revision before
reading test poses for scoring. The provided split exposes distinct sequence
animal keys but no global intruder identifiers. The image edges are only
computational reflection limits, not measured cage walls. Large tracking
jumps remain in recorded evaluation, and model fitting alone uses the frozen
50 px triplet rule. These limits, one dataset, and its CC-BY-NC-SA terms
constrain use and interpretation. A future commercial data application needs
a separate rights decision or another source.
