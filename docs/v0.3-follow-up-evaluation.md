# Proposed v0.3 follow-up: frozen-model transfer audit

**Status:** design and source-metadata screening only. No pose outcomes from
this candidate cohort have been loaded or measured. This is a new study, not a
revision of the completed v0.3 preference rule.

## Question and interpretation

Do the path-length and boundary-occupancy discrepancies seen for the frozen
v0.3 M0 and M1 models recur in a separate, eligible open-field cohort? This is
an audit of model transfer to another recording source. M1 failed the v0.3
development rule and cannot be promoted by changing that decision after the
fact. The new cohort can describe robustness or failure under its own declared
conditions; it cannot establish a biological boundary mechanism.

## Candidate source and outcome-blind screen

The candidate is the ETH Zürich [open-field stress-model deposit](https://zenodo.org/records/8186065)
(`10.5281/zenodo.8186065`), separate from the Roche/Yohimbine deposit used in
v0.2 and v0.3. The published `METADATA.csv` is 55,649 bytes with MD5
`9d0373be620e20fad749d3e3a670a363`; the downloaded metadata matched that
MD5. It lists 411 recordings and 212 distinct `Animal` identifiers.

The metadata-only candidate subset is exactly `Condition=Control`,
`Treatment=Control`, `Experiment=TFS`, and `Session=OFT1`: 15 recordings from
15 distinct male animal identifiers. None of those identifiers is among the
eight Roche controls already used by Umwelt. Distinct identifiers and a
separate deposit do not alone prove independent acquisition or comparable
experimental conditions. The study methods and file-level data must be checked
before calling this an eligible transfer cohort.

## Eligibility gates before observing outcomes

1. Read the source methods and confirm what `OFT1`, `Control`, and `TFS` mean,
   including whether these recordings precede relevant manipulations.
2. Verify the pose archive against its published size and MD5, then verify the
   selected extracted files and preserve their checksums.
3. Inspect the source keypoint and landmark schema, coordinate axes, frame
   index, missingness representation, and animal/recording identity. Derive
   arena bounds only from supported source landmarks or documented geometry.
4. Determine whether camera scale and arena geometry permit direct transfer of
   pixel-valued fitted parameters. Any geometry-only conversion must be fixed
   before viewing trajectory outcomes and applied identically to M0 and M1.
5. Resolve repeat recordings and exclusions by animal identity using metadata
   and source-quality rules fixed before calculating model discrepancies. The
   planned subset stays the 15 `OFT1` recordings; failures are reported, not
   silently replaced from another session or treatment group.

If the schema, acquisition context, or scaling cannot support a comparable
test, report the failed gate and choose a new source in a separately dated
protocol. No outcome-guided adaptation of the current candidate is allowed.

## Proposed measurement contract

After the gates pass, freeze an executable protocol before comparing models:

- Unit of analysis: animal, one first-session recording per eligible animal.
  Synthetic replicates quantify model variability and do not increase the
  biological sample size.
- Models: final M0 and M1 parameter snapshots from the canonical v0.3 run;
  no fitting or tuning on the candidate cohort.
- Primary descriptive endpoint: per-animal median absolute relative path-length
  discrepancy for each model, with valid-transition exposure and the paired
  M1-minus-M0 difference.
- Secondary endpoints: adjacent-displacement and absolute-turning distances,
  boundary-band occupancy error, zone-conditioned positive-displacement
  distances, missingness, and outside-arena source quality.
- Pairing: identical frame-availability masks and derived seeds for M0/M1
  within each animal and replicate. Record both the per-animal results and
  summaries across animals; keep every negative or missing result visible.

The number of synthetic replicates, exact geometry conversion if needed, and
any exclusion thresholds must be serialized before accessing trajectory
outcomes. No retrospective pass/fail threshold is assigned to this transfer
audit. It does not revive the failed v0.3 M1 eligibility decision.

## Next implementation gate

Build a small source-inspection report for the 15 selected files, then publish
the finalized protocol and adapter separately. Only after those are reviewed
should the model comparison run. The raw source files remain outside Git;
licenses, authors, source checksums, and generated artifact hashes remain
explicit in the run provenance.
