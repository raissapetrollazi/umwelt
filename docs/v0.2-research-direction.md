# v0.2 Experiment - Spatial Mouse

## Status

Umwelt v0.2 implements the first complete spatial recorded-to-synthetic development workflow. It includes explicit spatial observations, a pinned Roche open-field adapter, image-space arena geometry, gap-aware trajectory measurements, a pre-declared evaluation protocol, a compact seeded movement baseline, replicated generation, descriptive recorded-versus-synthetic comparison, provenance, reporting, and artifact hashes.

Version 0.2 remains pre-alpha research software. It does not claim a validated biological model of mouse navigation, exploration, anxiety, motivation, intention, or decision-making. The two development animals provide descriptive model-development evidence rather than a confirmatory population test.

## Core question

> Can a compact, explicit generative model reproduce selected geometric and kinematic properties of recorded mouse trajectories in an open field?

The question is narrower than explaining why a mouse moves as it does. Correspondence in the selected measurements does not establish recovery of a biological mechanism.

## Source and provenance

The selected source is the Roche/ETH Zenodo deposit `10.5281/zenodo.8188683`, licensed CC BY 4.0. It contains 32 unique animals and recordings: eight `Control` records at dose `0` and eight `Yohimbine` records at each of doses `1`, `3`, and `6` mg/kg.

Umwelt pins the published metadata and pose-archive byte sizes and MD5 values. After the archive is verified, a 32-entry Umwelt manifest pins each extracted DeepLabCut CSV by exact frame count, byte size, and SHA-256. Those member hashes are derived Umwelt integrity facts, not checksums published by Zenodo.

The 13 mouse-body keypoints remain separate from the four arena landmarks `tl`, `tr`, `bl`, and `br`. Treatment and dosage remain source provenance and are not converted into behavioral causes or model state.

## Recorded trajectory contract

The recorded protocol selects the source `bodycentre` keypoint in the Roche `x-right-y-down` image frame measured in pixels. `minimum_likelihood` is explicitly `None`: the source material does not validate a cutoff, so Umwelt does not invent one. Source likelihood values remain available as provenance and quality information.

The protocol applies no interpolation or smoothing. Missing poses, missing coordinates, rejected coordinates, or skipped frame indices break continuity. Displacement requires accepted endpoints at adjacent frame indices. Exact zero displacement is retained and breaks the turning sequence. Heading is normalized counterclockwise from `+x` after correcting the image-axis sign convention.

The source does not establish a sampling rate, pixel-to-centimeter calibration, or physical arena dimensions. Therefore v0.2 does not report speed or physical distance.

## Arena and evaluation protocol

For each recording, the image-space rectangular arena is derived from the four source landmarks. Each side uses the median of the corresponding finite landmark coordinates across the recording. This produces a stable axis-aligned image-space rectangle without inventing physical geometry or a landmark-confidence cutoff.

The evaluation protocol is declared before baseline results and uses:

- total path length in pixels;
- empirical 1-Wasserstein distance for valid adjacent displacement distributions;
- empirical 1-Wasserstein distance for turning-angle distributions;
- empirical 1-Wasserstein distance for in-arena boundary-distance distributions;
- absolute difference in geometric center-region occupancy, where the center is the rectangle after a 25% wall margin on each side;
- total-variation distance over a fixed `4 x 4` image-space occupancy grid;
- absolute difference in the fraction of accepted positions falling outside the derived arena.

Recorded and synthetic trajectories are measured separately before comparison. No implicit scientific acceptance threshold is assigned. Speed and movement autocorrelation remain excluded because the source provides no validated time calibration.

## Generative baseline

The v0.2 baseline is `persistent-reflecting-random-walk-v1`. It is intentionally compact and inspectable rather than machine-learning based.

Fitting uses only the six fitting animals and estimates:

- probability of exact zero displacement among valid adjacent movements;
- a log-normal distribution for positive displacement magnitude;
- a zero-mean normal turning increment with dispersion estimated from recorded turns;
- a normalized pooled initial-position location within the recorded arenas.

Synthetic generation starts from the fitted normalized position, samples a seeded initial heading, preserves exact stationary steps probabilistically, samples positive displacement and turning increments, and reflects attempted movement at rectangular image-space boundaries before clamping to the valid arena. It generates new trajectories rather than replaying recorded coordinates.

The model contains no emotional, intentional, anxiety, curiosity, goal, or latent psychological variable. Boundary interaction is a computational movement rule, not a biological claim.

## Reproducible laboratory workflow

The `umwelt spatial` workflow:

1. validates the pinned Roche catalog and the eight control identities;
2. derives the recorded arenas and gap-aware fitting trajectories;
3. fits the pooled baseline on the six fitting animals only;
4. derives deterministic subject/replicate seeds from a master seed;
5. generates synthetic trajectories on each development recording's frame-index grid;
6. measures recorded and synthetic trajectories separately using the frozen protocol;
7. writes compact per-replicate comparisons;
8. writes resolved configuration, protocol, model, seeds, provenance, a readable report, and an artifact hash manifest.

Full synthetic replicate trajectories are intentionally discarded after compact evaluation. Existing output directories are not overwritten.

The user-facing configuration exposes only the data directory, master seed, replicate count, and output directory. Scientific choices that must remain pre-declared—the control split, position rule, evaluation protocol, and baseline family—are pinned in code rather than exposed as result-tunable configuration knobs.

## Interpretation boundary

A good geometric fit means only that the selected synthetic summaries resemble the selected recorded summaries under this development protocol. A poor fit is also a useful result because it identifies structure missing from the minimal baseline.

Center/periphery geometry must not be interpreted as anxiety, fear, confidence, preference, or motivation. Treatment metadata must not be used as a causal explanation. Synthetic state is not evidence about a real animal's subjective state or true mechanism.

## Relationship to later milestones

Version 0.2 intentionally stops before causal environmental interventions, social behavior, neural or physiological modalities, latent-state inference, counterfactual branching, multimodal integration, and a general physics engine. Those belong to later milestones only when supported by concrete experiments.

The v0.1 temporal and v0.2 spatial experiments use different datasets and scientific questions but share the same project principles: explicit provenance, strict validation, deterministic seeds, separate recorded and synthetic information categories, conservative claims, and preservation of negative or null results.
