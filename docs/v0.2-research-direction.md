# v0.2 Experiment - Spatial Mouse

## Status

Umwelt v0.2 is the first spatial milestone. This document defines the intended
scientific question, dataset boundary, information categories, evaluation plan,
and implementation constraints before spatial model code is introduced.

Version 0.2 does not claim a validated model of mouse navigation, exploration,
anxiety, motivation, or decision-making. It introduces an explicit spatial body
and world so that recorded and synthetic trajectories can be represented and
compared reproducibly.

## Core question

> Can a compact, explicit generative model reproduce selected geometric and
> kinematic properties of recorded mouse trajectories in an open field?

The question is deliberately narrower than explaining why a mouse moves as it
does. Version 0.2 asks whether a small executable movement model can reproduce
measured spatial structure. It does not infer intention or recover a biological
navigation mechanism.

## Selected source dataset

The planned source is the public Zenodo deposit:

- title: *Raw video and pose estimation data of top view open field mouse
  behavior recordings after yohimbine injections*;
- authors: Lukas M. von Ziegler, Fabienne K. Roessler, Oliver Sturman, Eoin C.
  O'Connor, and Johannes Bohacek;
- record DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.8188683`;
- source context: one Roche open-field experiment containing 32 mouse
  recordings under saline, 1 mg/kg, 3 mg/kg, or 6 mg/kg yohimbine conditions;
- selected archive: `data.zip`, containing DeepLabCut pose-estimation CSV data;
- published archive size: approximately 514 MB;
- published MD5 for `data.zip`: `8b48f2060146c03d578d5d6971aa70e9`;
- metadata file: `METADATA_ROCHE.csv`;
- data license: CC BY 4.0.

Raw videos are not required for the first v0.2 experiment and should not be
downloaded by default. The pose archive is sufficient for the planned
trajectory-first workflow if source metadata and coordinate semantics can be
validated by the adapter.

The dataset contains pharmacological treatment groups, but v0.2 is not a drug-
effect experiment. Treatment and dosage remain recorded provenance. They must
not be silently converted into behavioral causes or internal model variables.

## Primary analysis population

The first spatial baseline should prefer saline/control recordings so the core
movement experiment does not pool known pharmacological interventions by
default.

The exact subject identifiers and final training/development split are not
hard-coded in this direction document. The dataset adapter must first validate
the published metadata, determine whether animal identities are unique across
recordings, and expose treatment and animal identifiers explicitly.

The split must then follow these rules:

- split by animal identity rather than file identity when repeated recordings
  of one animal exist;
- never place observations from the same animal in both fitting and development
  evaluation;
- keep saline/control status explicit in provenance;
- do not tune the baseline against development trajectories and continue to
  call any exposed set development rather than pristine confirmatory test data;
- if the saline/control sample is too small for a defensible development split,
  stop and revise the experiment design rather than silently pooling treated
  animals.

The non-saline recordings may later support an explicitly declared
out-of-distribution diagnostic. They are not required for the first spatial
baseline and must not be used to claim causal treatment effects.

## Recorded information

The source pose files contain frame-indexed position estimates produced by
DeepLabCut. Umwelt should preserve the distinction between source observations
and quantities derived from them.

Recorded or source-derived input may include, when the adapter can verify the
corresponding source columns:

- frame index or source time coordinate;
- tracked body-part `(x, y)` coordinates;
- per-keypoint likelihood or confidence values;
- source animal and recording identifiers;
- experimental grouping metadata;
- missing or rejected pose estimates.

A DeepLabCut coordinate is a measurement produced by a pose-estimation system.
It is not a direct physical-state measurement and must retain that provenance.

## Derived spatial information

Quantities computed by Umwelt from accepted source coordinates are derived
measurements, not newly recorded observations. Planned derived quantities
include:

- representative body position;
- displacement between valid adjacent observations;
- path length;
- speed when a validated time scale is available;
- orientation or heading when supported by sufficient keypoints;
- turning angle;
- distance to arena boundaries when arena geometry is known;
- spatial occupancy summaries.

Gaps must remain explicit. Umwelt must not calculate displacement, speed, or
turning across missing intervals as if the trajectory were continuous.

Physical units must never be fabricated. If the source supports only pixel
coordinates, the first adapter may preserve pixel-space geometry. Conversion to
centimeters requires explicit calibration or documented arena geometry.

## Spatial representation

Version 0.2 should introduce a small headless spatial vocabulary only after this
experiment requires it. The initial implementation is expected to need:

- finite two-dimensional coordinates;
- an explicit coordinate frame and units;
- time-indexed spatial series with source category and gaps;
- a minimal body pose or representative position;
- explicit arena bounds;
- derived trajectory and kinematic measurements.

These concepts should not be coupled to a renderer. A complete v0.2 experiment
must be runnable and inspectable without graphics.

## Arena boundary

The open-field arena is part of the experimental context, not merely a drawing
surface. Umwelt should represent arena bounds explicitly once the source
geometry can be verified.

The implementation must not guess real-world dimensions from image dimensions.
If only image-space bounds are supported by the source, the model and metrics
must state that they operate in image coordinates.

Version 0.2 may use regions such as center and periphery only as deterministic
geometric partitions used for measurement. It must not interpret time in those
regions as anxiety, fear, confidence, preference, or motivation.

## Initial evaluation dimensions

Recorded and synthetic trajectories should be summarized separately before any
comparison. The first evaluation set should remain compact and inspectable.
Candidate dimensions are:

- total path length;
- displacement distribution;
- speed distribution where time calibration is valid;
- turning-angle distribution where orientation is valid;
- distance-to-boundary distribution;
- center/periphery occupancy under an explicitly defined geometric partition;
- coarse two-dimensional occupancy profile;
- movement autocorrelation at declared lags when sampling supports it.

The exact metric set should be declared before inspecting synthetic baseline
results. No implicit scientific acceptance threshold should be introduced.

## Minimal generative baseline

The first v0.2 model should be an explicit, seeded movement baseline rather than
machine learning. It should generate new trajectories rather than classify or
replay recorded ones.

A suitable minimal family may condition the next displacement on a small set of
observable quantities such as recent movement magnitude, recent heading, and
arena geometry. The exact mechanism belongs to the later modeling commit and
must be justified against the recorded measurements available from the selected
adapter.

The model must not contain variables named as emotions, intentions, anxiety,
curiosity, goals, or other psychological constructs merely to improve fit.

## Relationship to v0.1

Version 0.2 extends the laboratory architecture rather than pretending that the
COMPASS temporal experiment contains spatial information that was never
recorded.

The v0.1 sleep/activity models and the v0.2 spatial model may use different
source datasets, sampling rates, observation schemas, and scientific questions.
They should still share project principles:

- explicit source categories;
- strict validation;
- reproducible configuration;
- deterministic seed derivation;
- recorded and synthetic evaluation kept separate until comparison;
- provenance for every transformation;
- negative and null results preserved;
- model complexity added only in response to a defined question or documented
  baseline failure.

The v0.2 baseline does not need to combine sleep/activity state with movement.
Multimodal integration remains a later milestone unless a concrete experiment
requires it.

## Explicit non-goals for v0.2

Version 0.2 does not introduce:

- causal environmental interventions;
- food, shelter, light, or stimuli as decision-driving world state;
- social interaction between synthetic animals;
- pharmacological causal-effect modeling;
- neural or physiological modalities;
- latent mental-state inference;
- counterfactual branching;
- graphical rendering as a simulation requirement;
- a general physics engine;
- a claim that geometric similarity demonstrates biological mechanism recovery.

Those boundaries keep v0.2 distinct from the planned Behavioral World, Social
Animals, Neural Mouse, latent-state, and counterfactual milestones.

## Reproducibility requirements

A completed spatial run should preserve enough information to reconstruct what
was measured, fitted, generated, and compared. At minimum this is expected to
include:

- dataset DOI, authorship, license, selected file names, sizes, and published
  checksums;
- resolved recording/animal roles;
- source coordinate semantics and accepted keypoints;
- pose-confidence and missing-data rules;
- coordinate frame and units;
- arena geometry source;
- derived-measurement definitions;
- complete fitted model parameters and assumptions;
- master and derived seeds;
- recorded and synthetic metrics as separate categories;
- readable comparison and limitations;
- software version or Git revision;
- artifact hashes.

Downloaded source data remains local and should not be committed to the Umwelt
repository.

## Stop conditions during implementation

The implementation should stop for design review rather than inventing missing
scientific facts if any of the following occur:

- the published metadata cannot identify recordings and animal identities
  sufficiently to create a leakage-safe split;
- the pose files cannot be mapped to documented body parts consistently;
- sampling rate or time calibration required by a planned metric cannot be
  established;
- arena geometry required for boundary metrics cannot be verified;
- the selected dataset license or attribution requirements cannot be preserved;
- the saline/control subset is too small for the declared baseline design;
- a proposed abstraction requires assumptions not supported by the concrete
  experiment.

These are experiment-design failures to resolve explicitly, not conditions that
should be hidden by permissive parsing or guessed constants.
